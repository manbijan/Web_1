Why we roast in-house

Most cafés buy their beans from somewhere else, sitting in a warehouse for weeks before they reach a cup. We wanted something more direct — a roaster in the back, a bar up front, and about twelve feet between the two.

We buy in small lots from growers we've met, roast in batches under ten kilos, and rest each roast before it goes on bar. If a bag isn't right, we don't sell it. That's the whole philosophy.
